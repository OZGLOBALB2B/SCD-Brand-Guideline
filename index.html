Brand

Who we are

SCD stands for Semiconductor Devices Ltd. The company designs and manufactures cooled and uncooled infrared detectors and high-power laser diodes for defense, homeland security, commercial and space applications, and has been doing so for over four decades.

The spectrum bar

Three spectral bands, each with its own wavelength range and detector materials, sit on one continuous infrared scale. The bar under them uses an ironbow thermal gradient, read from short wavelengths on the left to long on the right. It is a website graphic device, not part of the brand palette.

Identity

Message

The key message of the brand. It leads the website and opens any main communication, set in Host Grotesk Medium in teal.

Clarity at the
Edge of Sight

The battlefield is dust, vibration, and split-second decisions. Our field-proven SWIR, MWIR, and LWIR detectors are engineered for the extremes to ensure you always stay a step ahead.

Primary colors

Primary colors are used together when discussing general topics. All colors, primary and secondary, can be used together for general topics, especially on merchandising items.
